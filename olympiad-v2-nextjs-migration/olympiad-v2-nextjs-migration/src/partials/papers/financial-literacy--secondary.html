Sample Paper 2 (Class 10)

  1. 1.Financial Literacy is best defined as:
    • a) Ability to read financial newspapers
    • b) Ability to understand how money works and make informed financial decisions
    • c) Skill of maintaining bank account only

  2. 2.Which of the following is NOT a component of personal financial management?
    • a) Budgeting
    • b) Investing
    • c) Borrowing
    • d) Guessing
  3. 3.A plan that helps you allocate income towards expenses, savings, and investments is called a:
    • a) Loan
    • b) Budget
    • c) Share
    • d) Bond

    • a) Dependent on others for money decisions
    • b) Self-sufficient in managing money
    • c) Over-confident investors

    • b) Better regulation of expenses and reduced financial stress
