Classes VI – VIII · Middle Division · Money Smart

Financial Literacy — Official Sample Question Paper

  1. 1.The primary purpose of money is to:
    • a) Make goods more expensive
    • b) Facilitate the exchange of goods and services
    • c) Eliminate savings
    • d) Increase borrowing
  2. 2.Which of the following is an example of money?
    • a) Currency note
    • b) School uniform
    • c) Furniture
    • d) Food
  3. 3.The system in which goods are exchanged directly for other goods is called:
    • a) Banking
    • b) Barter
    • c) Investment
    • d) Budgeting
  4. 4.Which of the following is generally a need?
    • a) Designer watch
    • b) Basic food
    • c) Video game
    • d) Luxury car
  5. 5.Smart spending means:
    • a) Buying everything we like
    • b) Spending without planning
    • c) Comparing choices and spending according to priorities
    • d) Spending all our savings
  6. 6.Before buying an expensive product, a wise consumer should first:
    • a) Buy immediately
    • b) Borrow money
    • c) Compare prices, quality and need
    • d) Ignore the cost
  7. 7.Money received from a job or business is generally called:
    • a) Expense
    • b) Income
    • c) Loan
    • d) Discount
  8. 8.Which of the following can be a source of income?
    • a) Salary
    • b) Electricity bill
    • c) Rent paid
    • d) Grocery expense
  9. 9.Which statement is correct?
    • a) Income is always greater than expenses
    • b) Income is money received, while expenses are money spent
    • c) Expenses are a type of savings
    • d) Income and expenses mean the same thing
  10. 10.Saving means:
    • a) Spending all income
    • b) Keeping aside part of income for future use
    • c) Borrowing money
    • d) Buying unnecessary products
  11. 11.Which is the best example of a financial goal?
    • a) “I want to spend more.”
    • b) “I will save ₹2,000 in four months for a bicycle.”
    • c) “I will buy things whenever I want.”
    • d) “I will not keep track of my money.”
  12. 12.A short-term financial goal could be:
    • a) Saving for a purchase needed within a few months
    • b) Planning retirement
    • c) Buying a house after 20 years
    • d) Building a large investment portfolio
  13. 13.A bank is an institution that provides services such as:
    • a) Accepting deposits and providing loans
    • b) Manufacturing school books
    • c) Producing food
    • d) Selling only clothes
  14. 14.Money deposited in a bank can generally be:
    • a) Kept safely and accessed according to account rules
    • b) Used by anyone
    • c) Automatically doubled
    • d) Lost immediately
  15. 15.Which account is commonly used by individuals for regular banking transactions?
    • a) Savings account
    • b) Examination account
    • c) Shopping account
    • d) School account
  16. 16.A budget is a plan for:
    • a) Income and expenses
    • b) Only shopping
    • c) Only borrowing
    • d) Only investing
  17. 17.If a student receives ₹1,000 and plans to save ₹300, how much can be planned for spending?
    • a) ₹300
    • b) ₹500
    • c) ₹700
    • d) ₹1,300
  18. 18.Which practice helps in better money management?
    • a) Recording expenses
    • b) Ignoring expenses
    • c) Spending without limits
    • d) Borrowing for every purchase
  19. 19.Which of the following is a digital payment method?
    • a) UPI
    • b) Barter
    • c) Cheque book only
    • d) Cash exchange
  20. 20.A person should never share their OTP or PIN with:
    • a) Anyone
    • b) Their own memory
    • c) Their personal diary
    • d) Nobody except themselves
  21. 21.If someone asks for your banking PIN over a phone call, you should:
    • a) Share it immediately
    • b) Refuse and report the suspicious request
    • c) Send it by message
    • d) Post it online
  22. 22.Investment means:
    • a) Putting money into something with the expectation of future benefit
    • b) Spending money on food
    • c) Paying a school fee
    • d) Buying daily necessities
  23. 23.Which of the following is an investment product?
    • a) Mutual fund
    • b) School bag
    • c) Restaurant bill
    • d) Bus ticket
  24. 24.Before making an investment, a person should consider:
    • a) Risk and expected return
    • b) Only advertisements
    • c) Only friends' opinions
    • d) Only the colour of the product
  25. 25.Investment risk refers to the possibility that:
    • a) An investment may not perform as expected
    • b) Money will always double
    • c) Returns are guaranteed
    • d) Prices can never change
  26. 26.Generally, higher potential returns are associated with:
    • a) No risk at all
    • b) Higher potential risk
    • c) Guaranteed profit
    • d) Zero uncertainty
  27. 27.Insurance primarily helps people to:
    • a) Manage certain financial risks
    • b) Guarantee investment profits
    • c) Avoid all expenses
    • d) Double their savings
  28. 28.A financial market is a place or system where:
    • a) Financial assets and securities are bought and sold
    • b) Only household goods are sold
    • c) Only food is exchanged
    • d) School uniforms are manufactured
  29. 29.Which organisation regulates the securities market in India?
    • a) SEBI
    • b) RBI
    • c) SBI
    • d) NSDL
  30. 30.Which is the most responsible approach to investing?
    • a) Invest because a friend recommends it
    • b) Invest without understanding the product
    • c) Understand the product, risk and objective before investing
    • d) Believe every online investment advertisement
