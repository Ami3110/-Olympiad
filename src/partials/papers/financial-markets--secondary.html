Classes IX – X · Secondary Division · NSQF Level 1

Financial Markets — Official Sample Question Paper

  1. 1.Which of the following best describes money?
    • a) A product that can only be consumed
    • b) Anything generally accepted as a means of payment
    • c) A document issued only by banks
    • d) A form of investment
  2. 2.Before money was commonly used, people mainly exchanged goods through:
    • a) Online banking
    • b) Barter system
    • c) Stock exchanges
    • d) Digital wallets
  3. 3.A major problem with the barter system is:
    • a) It requires banks
    • b) It requires a double coincidence of wants
    • c) It uses too much currency
    • d) It guarantees equal value
  4. 4.Which of the following is an important characteristic of good money?
    • a) It should be difficult to carry
    • b) It should have limited acceptability
    • c) It should be durable
    • d) It should lose value quickly
  5. 5.Financial planning primarily means:
    • a) Spending all available income
    • b) Planning how to manage income, expenses, savings and investments
    • c) Borrowing money regularly
    • d) Avoiding banks completely
  6. 6.Money received from salary, wages, business or other sources is called:
    • a) Expense
    • b) Income
    • c) Liability
    • d) Investment
  7. 7.Which of the following is an example of an expense?
    • a) Salary received
    • b) Interest earned
    • c) Rent paid
    • d) Dividend received
  8. 8.The primary function of a bank is to:
    • a) Manufacture goods
    • b) Accept deposits and provide financial services
    • c) Sell household products
    • d) Produce currency notes independently
  9. 9.Why is saving money important?
    • a) It increases unnecessary spending
    • b) It helps meet future needs and emergencies
    • c) It eliminates the need for financial planning
    • d) It guarantees high investment returns
  10. 10.Which of the following is the best example of a financial goal?
    • a) “I will spend more money.”
    • b) “I want to save ₹12,000 for a laptop in one year.”
    • c) “I will buy something whenever I want.”
    • d) “I do not want to keep any records.”
  11. 11.Systematic saving means:
    • a) Saving only when extra money is available
    • b) Saving a fixed or planned amount regularly
    • c) Spending first and saving what remains
    • d) Borrowing regularly
  12. 12.A budget helps a person to:
    • a) Track and plan income and expenses
    • b) Increase expenses automatically
    • c) Avoid setting financial goals
    • d) Eliminate all financial risks
  13. 13.Investment generally means:
    • a) Using money only for daily expenses
    • b) Putting money into an asset or financial product with the expectation of future benefit
    • c) Keeping all money at home
    • d) Giving money away without expectation of benefit
  14. 14.Which of the following is generally considered an investment option?
    • a) Equity shares
    • b) Grocery bills
    • c) Electricity charges
    • d) Bus fare
  15. 15.Securities are best described as:
    • a) Financial instruments representing certain rights or claims
    • b) Physical security guards
    • c) Household items
    • d) Bank buildings
  16. 16.Which of the following is an example of a security?
    • a) Equity share
    • b) School uniform
    • c) Grocery coupon
    • d) Bus ticket
  17. 17.The market where securities are offered to investors for the first time is called the:
    • a) Secondary market
    • b) Primary market
    • c) Retail market
    • d) Barter market
  18. 18.When a company issues shares to the public for the first time through an IPO, it is participating in the:
    • a) Primary market
    • b) Secondary market
    • c) Commodity shop
    • d) Money exchange counter
  19. 19.The secondary market mainly facilitates:
    • a) The first issue of securities by companies
    • b) Buying and selling of existing securities among investors
    • c) Printing of currency
    • d) Preparation of household budgets
  20. 20.Which institution is an example of a stock exchange in India?
    • a) RBI
    • b) NSE
    • c) SBI
    • d) LIC
  21. 21.A derivative is a financial instrument whose value is derived from:
    • a) A school certificate
    • b) An underlying asset or reference
    • c) A bank building
    • d) A currency note only
  22. 22.Which of the following can be an underlying asset for a derivative?
    • a) Stock
    • b) School bag
    • c) Furniture
    • d) Notebook
  23. 23.One important purpose of derivatives is:
    • a) Risk management or hedging
    • b) Avoiding all financial transactions
    • c) Printing money
    • d) Preparing a household budget
  24. 24.A depository primarily helps investors by:
    • a) Holding securities in electronic form
    • b) Printing currency notes
    • c) Providing school education
    • d) Selling insurance products directly
  25. 25.Which of the following is an Indian depository?
    • a) NSDL
    • b) NSE
    • c) RBI
    • d) SEBI
  26. 26.A Demat account is mainly used to:
    • a) Store physical cash
    • b) Hold securities in electronic form
    • c) Prepare income-tax returns only
    • d) Receive salary only
  27. 27.Which organisation regulates the securities market in India?
    • a) RBI
    • b) SEBI
    • c) SBI
    • d) NSDL
  28. 28.An investor buys shares of a listed company from another investor through a stock exchange. This transaction takes place in the:
    • a) Primary market
    • b) Secondary market
    • c) Barter market
    • d) Wholesale goods market
  29. 29.Which statement about investment is most appropriate?
    • a) All investments provide guaranteed returns
    • b) Higher potential returns may involve higher risk
    • c) Investment never involves risk
    • d) Investors should always invest without research
  30. 30.Which sequence correctly represents a basic financial planning process?
    • a) Spend → Borrow → Ignore expenses → Invest
    • b) Set goals → Prepare a budget → Save systematically → Invest appropriately

    • d) Borrow → Spend → Save → Set goals
